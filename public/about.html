What it does

Everything you need to read your library. Nothing you don’t.

Right where you left off

Keep Reading, Recently Added, Readlist and Finished shelves on the home screen.

Syncs with your server

Reading progress, favorites and read status sync both ways with Jellyfin.

Type the way you like it

Eleven fonts built into iOS, plus size, spacing, margins, justification and paged or scrolling layout.

Ready for manga

Right-to-left reading, and page dimming so scanned pages don’t glare at night.

Notes that stay put

Bookmarks, highlights and notes come back on the exact page when you return.

Find anything

Search your whole server. Sort and filter by favorites, unread, in progress or downloaded.

Easy to navigate

Table of contents, a scrubber with haptic feedback, and pages left in the chapter.

Feels like iOS

Liquid Glass on iOS 26, SF Symbols, haptics, and light and dark mode.

Six reading themes

A page for every kind of light.

From bright white to a dim Night theme for reading in bed, plus Bold for extra-heavy text.

Offline

Take your library on a plane.

  • Download any book with one tap. You’ll see live progress and the file size.
  • Drop your own EPUBs and PDFs into the JellyShelf folder in the Files app and they appear on your shelf.
  • Titles and authors are read from filenames like Austen, Jane - Emma.epub.

Private by design

Your reading stays yours.

  • No JellyShelf account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your place in each book, bookmarks, highlights and settings are saved on your device, so the app opens instantly.
  • The app only talks to the server you sign into. Progress syncs back whenever that server is reachable.
