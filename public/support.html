Getting started

  1. Install the Bookshelf pluginIn the Jellyfin dashboard, go to Plugins → Catalog, install Bookshelf, then restart your server.
  2. Add a books libraryGo to Dashboard → Libraries and add a library with the content type Books that points at your EPUB, PDF, CBZ and CBR files. Let the scan finish.
  3. Sign in to Self-ShelfEnter your server address, like jellyfin.home.lan:8096, then your username and password. You can also use Quick Connect.
  4. Start readingYour shelves fill in right away. Tap a book to open it, or download it to read offline.

Connecting to your server

Self-Shelf can’t find my server
  • Use the same address you use to open Jellyfin in a browser, including the port (the default is 8096).
  • If your server uses a custom port or HTTPS, include http:// or https:// at the start.
  • A local address like 192.168.1.20:8096 only works on the same Wi‑Fi network as your server. Away from home, use your server’s public or VPN address.
  • On iPhone and iPad, make sure Self-Shelf is allowed to reach your network: Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network → Self-Shelf.

Pasting your Jellyfin web address (ending in /web) is fine. Self-Shelf removes that part for you.

The web version says my server is blocked

The web version is served over HTTPS, and browsers won’t let an HTTPS page talk to a server on plain http://. Use your server’s https:// address, for example through a reverse proxy. The iPhone and iPad app doesn’t have this restriction.

How do I sign in with Quick Connect?

First turn on Quick Connect in your Jellyfin dashboard under Dashboard → General. Then choose Quick Connect on the Self-Shelf sign-in screen. You’ll get a short code. Enter it in Jellyfin on a device where you’re already signed in (under your profile → Quick Connect), and Self-Shelf signs in automatically.

I signed in, but my books aren’t showing up
  • Check that the Bookshelf plugin is installed and that your server was restarted afterward.
  • Make sure the library’s content type is set to Books, then run a library scan.
  • Check that your Jellyfin user has access to that library under Dashboard → Users → Library Access.

Reading and syncing

Which file formats are supported?

EPUB, PDF, CBZ and CBR. For manga, switch the reading direction to Right to Left in the reader’s appearance settings.

What syncs with Jellyfin?

Your reading progress, favorites and read status sync with your server, so they carry over to other Jellyfin apps. If you read offline, your progress is sent up the next time your server is reachable.

Bookmarks, highlights, notes and your type settings are stored only on the device you made them on.

How do I change the font, size or theme?

While reading, tap the middle of the page to show the controls, then open the appearance menu. You can pick one of six themes (Original, Paper, Sepia, Bold, Quiet and Night), one of eleven fonts, the text size, line spacing, margins, justification, and paged or scrolling layout.

Offline and your own files

How do I read without an internet connection?

Open a book and tap Download. You’ll see its progress and file size. Downloaded books open without a connection, and you can find them all with the Downloaded filter in your library.

Can I add books that aren’t on my server?

Yes. On iPhone or iPad, open the Files app and go to On My iPhone (or On My iPad) → Self-Shelf. Put EPUB or PDF files there and they’ll appear on your shelf.

Name files like Austen, Jane - Emma.epub and Self-Shelf will fill in the title and author for you. To add a cover and details, press and hold the book and choose Find Metadata…

Privacy

What data does Self-Shelf collect?

None. Self-Shelf has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don’t run a server that sees your data.

  • The app connects directly to the Jellyfin server you sign into. Your sign-in token is stored in the device’s secure keychain.
  • Reading positions, bookmarks, highlights, notes, downloads and settings are stored on your device.
  • If you choose to look up details for a book you added yourself, the search text is sent to Open Library, then to Google Books if Open Library has no match. Nothing else is shared.
How do I delete my data?

Signing out in Settings removes downloads and reading positions from your device. Deleting the app removes everything else. Anything synced to your Jellyfin server is under your control there.

Is Self-Shelf made by Jellyfin?

No. Self-Shelf is an independent app and isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by the Jellyfin project. It uses Jellyfin’s public API to read the library you already have.

Reporting a problem

The quickest way to get something fixed is to open an issue on GitHub. It helps a lot if you include:

  • Your Self-Shelf version (Settings → Self-Shelf)
  • Your device and iOS version, or your browser if you’re using the web version
  • Your Jellyfin server version and Bookshelf plugin version
  • The file format (EPUB, PDF, CBZ or CBR), and whether it happens with every book or just one
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead, with a screenshot if you can
