DESCRIPTION (max 4000)
----------------------
JellyShelf is a native reader for the books, comics and PDFs in your Jellyfin library.

Jellyfin is great at storing your library. It is not great at reading it on a phone. JellyShelf signs into your server, pulls down your shelf, and gets out of the way so you can read.

Everything is local-first. Your place in a book, your bookmarks, highlights and type settings are stored on your device, so the app opens instantly and works on a plane. Progress is sent back to Jellyfin whenever your server is reachable.

YOUR LIBRARY, THE WAY YOU LEFT IT
• Keep Reading, Recently Added, Readlist and Finished shelves
• A full library grid you can sort by title, date added, rating or year
• Filters for favorites, unread, in progress and downloaded
• Search across your whole server
• Favorites and read status sync both ways with Jellyfin

A READER WORTH USING
• EPUB, PDF, CBZ and CBR support
• Six themes: Original, Paper, Sepia, Bold, Quiet and Night
• Page dimming for scanned PDFs
• Eleven fonts built into iOS, so nothing needs downloading
• Adjust type size, line spacing, margins and justification
• Choose paged or scrolling layout
• Right-to-left reading for manga
• Bookmarks, highlights and notes, restored right where you left them
• Table of contents, a page scrubber with haptic feedback, and pages left in the chapter
• The screen stays awake while you read

OFFLINE AND ON YOUR DEVICE
• Download any book for offline reading, with live progress and file size shown
• Drop your own EPUBs and PDFs into the JellyShelf folder in the Files app and they appear on your shelf
• Titles and authors are read from filenames like "Austen, Jane - Emma.epub"

BUILT FOR iPHONE AND iPAD
• Liquid Glass design on iOS 26
• A native tab bar that tucks away as you scroll
• SF Symbols, haptics, and light and dark mode

SIGN IN YOUR WAY
Sign in with a username and password, or use Quick Connect if your server has it enabled.

REQUIREMENTS
JellyShelf needs a Jellyfin server with the Bookshelf plugin installed. It doesn't host or sell any content. It reads the library you already have.

JellyShelf is an independent app and isn't affiliated with the Jellyfin project.
